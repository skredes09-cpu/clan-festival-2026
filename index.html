Eligibility & Rules

Simple. Fair. Competitive.

01 · TH10+

Members should be Town Hall 10 or higher, with troops and base at least 85% max for the applicable level.

02 · Verified members

Registration is reviewed before a player becomes an official festival participant.

03 · Honest scoring

Points are recorded through approved festival scoring. Suspicious or manipulated results may be rejected.

04 · Final approval

Leaderboard publication and final awards are subject to admin review and tie-break rules.
